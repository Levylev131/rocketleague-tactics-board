My Templates

Click a team button to drop a car on the pitch, then drag it into position. Select a car and drag the small dot above it to set which way it's facing. Select a car to reveal the Selected Car bar: pick 1, 2, or 3 to badge it (or just press that number key), and pick a highlight color to glow-ring it — the highlight sits around the car, so its blue/orange team color always stays visible underneath. Click Add Text to drop a label — drag the dot above it to rotate, drag the small square at its corner to resize, and double-click it to edit the words (select it and press Delete to remove it). Click a token to select it and press Delete (or double-click it) to remove it. Every possible kickoff spawn spot is marked with a dim dot — drag a car near one and it snaps into place. Click Draw Arrow, then drag on the pitch to draw a straight arrow (click it again to stop drawing); click an arrow to select it, or double-click to remove it. Tweak any formation, then click Save as Template to store your own version under My Templates (saved in this browser; click the × on a template to delete it). Use New Group to make a folder for templates, then drag any template into a group (or drag onto another template to reorder). Drag a group's header onto another group to reorder groups; deleting a group just ungroups its templates. Use Export Templates to download all your templates as a file you can back up, and Import Templates to load them back in on this device or any other.
